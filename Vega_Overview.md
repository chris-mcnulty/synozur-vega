## What Vega Does

Most organizations have a strategy. Few have a reliable way to connect that strategy to the day-to-day work of their teams. Goals get set at the start of the year, buried in decks, and revisited only when things have already gone off track. Vega fixes that.

Vega is a **Company Operating System** — a single platform that takes an organization from its foundational purpose all the way through to weekly team execution, with AI woven throughout to accelerate decisions and surface what matters most.

### The Five Layers of Vega

**1. Foundations**
Every strategy needs grounding. Vega starts by capturing the organization's mission, vision, core values, and long-horizon ambitions (3–5 year targets). These aren't static slides — they are live anchors that every goal and initiative links back to, so teams always understand the *why* behind their work.

**2. Strategy**
Vega helps leadership draft and maintain a living strategy. AI can generate strategy drafts from existing documents, suggest how goals align to the mission, and flag gaps between what the organization says it values and what it is actually pursuing. Strategies connect directly to outcomes, so there is never a question of whether execution reflects intent.

**3. Outcomes (OKRs)**
The core of execution lives here. Vega manages a full hierarchy of Objectives and Key Results — from organization-level goals down to team and individual targets. "Big Rocks" (the critical quarterly priorities) sit alongside OKRs, giving teams a clear, short-horizon focus that connects up to the annual plan. AI monitors pace and velocity in real time, forecasting whether each objective will land on time and flagging risks before they become problems.

**4. Focus Rhythm**
Strategy and goals mean nothing without a disciplined cadence of review. Vega's Focus Rhythm module turns recurring meetings into structured, productive conversations: live meeting mode with agenda timers, one-click capture of decisions and risks, attendee tracking, and automatic carryforward of open items. Meeting notes and context persist server-side, so anyone can rejoin mid-session and pick up exactly where the group left off.

**5. Reporting & Visibility**
Executives, team leads, and individual contributors each get a tailored dashboard — Company OS overview, Executive analytics with trend projections, and Team weekly focus views. Embeddable cards let leaders drop live OKR status directly into SharePoint, Galaxy portals, or any intranet without requiring every stakeholder to log in. Weekly AI-written digest emails keep opted-in users informed without requiring them to open the app.

### Built for How Organizations Actually Work

Vega integrates natively with **Microsoft 365** — syncing tasks bidirectionally with Microsoft Planner, surfacing calendar context in meeting prep, and connecting to Excel data sources for KR tracking. Single sign-on via Microsoft Entra ID means no separate credentials for organizations already on M365.

For organizations using **Synozur's Galaxy** customer portal, Vega provides a secure, read-only window into OKR progress — no separate login required for portal users.

A built-in **M365 Copilot Agent** lets users query Vega's data directly from Microsoft Teams and Copilot chat using natural language.

## What Outcomes to Expect

### Strategic Clarity Across the Organization
Within the first quarter, every team member should be able to answer three questions without opening a slide deck: What is this organization trying to achieve this year? What is my team's role in that? What am I personally accountable for this quarter? Vega makes those answers findable in under 30 seconds.

### Faster, Better-Informed Decisions
Vega's AI surfaces pace data, risk flags, and trend analysis continuously — not just at quarterly reviews. Leaders stop discovering problems after the fact and start intervening while there is still time to course-correct. Meeting prep that previously took an hour gets handled in minutes, with AI-generated briefings that pull context from live OKR data.

### A Cadence That Actually Holds
Organizations that use Vega's Focus Rhythm report shorter, more decisive meetings because the structure is built in. Decisions get captured. Action items get assigned. Progress carries forward automatically. The meeting itself becomes an accountability tool rather than a status report.

### Alignment That Survives Leadership Changes
When a key person leaves or a team restructures, Vega's bulk reassignment, hierarchy tools, and audit logging ensure continuity. Strategic context doesn't walk out the door with any single individual.

### Measurable Progress on Annual Goals
By the end of the first full annual cycle, organizations have a complete historical record: every check-in, every forecast, every period close-out. That data becomes the foundation for next year's planning — grounded in what actually happened, not what was hoped for.

## Who It Is For

Vega is designed for **growth-stage and mid-market organizations** (typically 50–2,000 employees) that have outgrown spreadsheets and slide decks but find enterprise strategy tools too rigid or too expensive. It supports both **consultant-led implementations** (where a Synozur partner guides setup and adoption) and **self-service onboarding** for teams ready to move on their own.

Roles supported include executives setting organizational direction, operations leaders managing cross-functional delivery, team managers running weekly rhythms, and individual contributors tracking their own commitments — each with permissions and dashboards calibrated to what they actually need to see.
